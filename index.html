Questions, answered

Anything else? Write to hello@agentflow.example.

Do I need to write prompts?

No. Describe the outcome in plain language and the agents plan the steps themselves. You can still give detailed instructions when you want to.

Can agents spend money or send emails without me?

Only if you allow it. Approval rules cover sending, publishing and payments by default, and you can tighten them per agent.

What happens to my data?

Your data is never used to train models. You can delete workspaces and history at any time, and export everything first.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel in two clicks from settings. Your agents stop at the end of the billing period and your data stays exportable for 30 days.
